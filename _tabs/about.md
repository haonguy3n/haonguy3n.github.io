I'm **Hao Nguyen**, an embedded software engineer. Born 17 November 2000, based in Vietnam.

I like problems where software has to respect physics: limited RAM, a watchdog that will reset
you, an interrupt that fires at the worst possible moment. Getting a system to behave correctly
under those constraints — and to keep behaving correctly at 3 a.m. six months later — is the part
of this job I enjoy most.

## What I do

- **Firmware development** in C and C++ — bare-metal and on top of an RTOS.
- **Peripheral and driver work** — I2C, SPI, UART, CAN, GPIO, ADC, timers, DMA.
- **Hardware bring-up and debugging** — JTAG/SWD, logic analyzer, oscilloscope, and a lot of
  reading datasheets.
- **Tooling and test** — Python scripts, host-side unit tests, and CI that builds for the target.
